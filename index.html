CHAPTER 6 · THE DECISION

Why act now

Every quarter of delay leaves the 9800 at 10 Gbps under TP+TLS — just as AI drives both more encrypted traffic and faster, AI-generated attacks, making "turn off inspection to save throughput" more dangerous than ever — and pushes the launch further from the next renewal window. That is the cost of waiting.

01
The customer already feels the gap

Today the customer must choose between reducing TLS Inspection, adding boxes with Maestro, or moving to the 29100/29200. None of these gives them a simple upgrade of the existing box — and with AI-driven threats escalating, reducing TLS Inspection is the one option they can least afford.

02
The upgrade window is time-limited

9700/9800 customers are entering renewal and capacity-planning cycles. Without an Upgrade SKU in time, the sales team arrives at the conversation with no competitive answer, and falls back on a discount, higher TCO, or a hardware swap.

03
Development time does not shrink if we wait

The road to GA takes about 18 months. A one-quarter delay does not buy time; it pushes the revenue and the market window out by another quarter.
